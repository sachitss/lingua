# Lingua v0.3.0 — Korean and Japanese

Released 30 Oct 2026 (build 7). **Preview release:** Italian, French and Japanese content still needs review by native speakers before the store release.

### What's new
- **Korean (한국어):** learn Korean from any of the other languages, or use Lingua with a Korean interface. All 227 words and phrases, example sentences, reading texts, conversations, grammar (polite present, 이에요/예요, particles) and numbers.
- **Japanese (日本語):** new. 227 words and phrases with romaji and example sentences, six reading texts, six conversations, three grammar topics (ます form, past ました, particles は・が・を・に・で) and numbers up to a million (いち, に, さん … ひゃくまん). The interface is available in Japanese too.
- **Native voices for every language (Azure neural voices):**
  - New: Italian (Elsa, Diego), French (Denise, Henri) and Japanese (Nanami, Keita).
  - Already included: German (Katja, Conrad), English (Sonia, Ryan), Spanish (Elvira, Álvaro), Nepali (Hemkala, Sagar) and Korean (SunHi, InJoon).
  - Female and male voice for every word, sentence, text, number and conversation line. Works offline.
- **Romanisation** for Korean, Japanese and Nepali can be switched on in the profile.
- Eight languages in total: English, German, Spanish, Italian, French, Korean, Japanese and Nepali.
- Version 0.3.0, build 7, on all platforms.
