# Lingua v0.4.0 — Greek

Released 30 Oct 2026 (build 8). **Preview release:** Italian, French, Japanese and Greek content still needs review by native speakers before the store release.

### What's new
- **Greek (Ελληνικά):** new. 227 words and phrases (nouns with their article) with romanisation and example sentences, six reading texts, six conversations, three grammar topics (είμαι/έχω, present tense, articles ο/η/το) and numbers up to a million. The interface is available in Greek too.
- **Native Greek voices:** Athina (female) and Nestoras (male), Azure neural voices, 621 recordings each.
- Nine languages in total: English, German, Spanish, Italian, French, Greek, Korean, Japanese and Nepali, each with a built-in female and male voice.
- Includes everything from [v0.3.0](../v0.3.0/RELEASE_NOTES.md): Korean, Japanese and the Azure voices for Italian, French and Japanese.
- Version 0.4.0, build 8, on all platforms.
