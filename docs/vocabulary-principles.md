# Vocabulary principles

Vocabulary is the core of all four language skills. Lingua's learning engine turns the four principles below into hard rules; see [Learning engine](architecture.md#learning-engine).

## Why vocabulary drives the four skills

| Skill | What vocabulary does |
| --- | --- |
| Listening | Lets the brain decode spoken words and grasp meaning in real time |
| Reading | Word recognition and context determine how well a text is understood |
| Speaking | Known active words let learners form fluent sentences without long pauses |
| Writing | A varied vocabulary lets learners express thoughts, details and arguments clearly |

## The four principles

1. **Learn words in context.** Study words inside full sentences, not as isolated lists.
2. **Read widely.** Regular reading exposes learners to new words naturally.
3. **Keep an active notebook.** Track new words with definitions, example sentences and synonyms.
4. **Practise active recall.** Translate or say sentences aloud to move words from recognition into real use.
