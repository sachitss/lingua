Lingua helps you learn English, German, Spanish, Italian, French and Nepali – in short daily sessions, with clear audio and pronunciation practice. Learn from any of the six languages into any other: for example German from English, Italian from Spanish, or Nepali from German.

TODAY'S PLAN
Choose 10 to 60 minutes a day. Lingua mixes words that are due for review, new words and pronunciation practice into one session, so you always know what to do next.

WORDS AND PHRASES IN CONTEXT
• 227 words and phrases for everyday life, from Beginner to Advanced
• Example sentences, so you learn words the way they are used
• Search, filter and keep your own notebook of words
• Word of the day on the home screen

REMEMBER WHAT YOU LEARN
Lingua uses FSRS spaced repetition, a modern scheduling method that brings each word back just before you would forget it. A word only counts as mastered after you have recalled it actively several times, over several days.

LISTEN AND SPEAK
• Listen to every word and example, normal or slow
• Record yourself and compare your pronunciation with the reference
• Practise short conversations by choosing or speaking your answer

TRAVEL PHRASES
"Learn before my trip": 40 phrases for the airport, immigration, the hotel and more, as a short course you can finish before you leave.

ALSO INCLUDED
• Numbers, with practice up to one million
• Grammar explanations with short quizzes
• Progress: daily minutes, streak, mastered words and the four skills
• Interface in English, German, Spanish, Italian, French and Nepali
• Daily reminders, if you want them

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device, and voice recordings are deleted when you close the app.
